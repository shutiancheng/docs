# Quickstart

Get your AI voice agent up and running in just a few steps.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A Renchi AI account ([sign up here](https://app.renchi.ai))
- A Twilio account with a phone number
- An ElevenLabs account for voice AI

## Step 1: Connect Your Phone Number

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations**
2. Click **Connect Twilio**
3. Enter your Twilio Account SID and Auth Token
4. Select the phone number to use for your AI agent

## Step 2: Configure Your Agent

1. Navigate to **Agents** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Agent**
3. Fill in the basic details:
   - **Name**: What customers will hear (e.g., "Sarah")
   - **Greeting**: How your agent answers calls
   - **Persona**: The tone and style of conversation

## Step 3: Set Business Hours

1. In your agent settings, go to the **Business Hours** tab
2. Set your operating hours for each day
3. Configure an after-hours greeting for calls outside business hours

## Step 4: Add Your Services

1. Go to the **Services** tab
2. Add the services you offer (e.g., "Boiler Repair", "Annual Service")
3. Set pricing and availability for each service

## Step 5: Test Your Agent

1. Call your connected phone number
2. Have a conversation with your AI agent
3. Check the **Conversations** page to review the transcript
