# How It Works

Renchi AI combines several technologies to deliver intelligent voice call handling.

## System Architecture

```
INBOUND CALL FLOW
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Customer Call → Twilio → ElevenLabs AI → Webhook Events
                                                ↓
                              ┌─────────────────┼─────────────────┐
                              ↓                 ↓                 ↓
                        Transcription    Audio Recording   Personalization

BACKEND SERVICES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Next.js API Routes
       ├── Supabase (Database + Storage)
       ├── Email Notifications
       └── Third-party Integrations
```

## Key Components

### Twilio (Telephony)

Twilio handles the phone infrastructure:
- Receives inbound calls to your business number
- Routes calls to the AI agent
- Manages call recording and status updates

### ElevenLabs (Voice AI)

ElevenLabs powers the conversational AI:
- Natural-sounding voice synthesis
- Real-time speech recognition
- Context-aware responses
- Handles complex multi-turn conversations

### Supabase (Database)

Supabase stores all your data:
- Customer records and history
- Conversation transcripts
- Job and booking details
- Team and organization settings

## Call Flow in Detail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Call Received">
    Customer dials your business number. Twilio receives the call and triggers a webhook to your Renchi AI backend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Agent Activated">
    The backend connects the call to your configured ElevenLabs agent, which greets the caller.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conversation">
    The AI agent handles the conversation, understanding intent, asking clarifying questions, and taking appropriate actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Actions Taken">
    Based on the conversation, the agent can book appointments, schedule callbacks, transfer calls, or resolve inquiries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-Call Processing">
    After the call ends, webhooks deliver the transcript, recording, and AI analysis for review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data Flow

| Event | Source | Destination | Data |
|-------|--------|-------------|------|
| Call started | Twilio | Backend | Caller ID, phone line |
| Conversation | ElevenLabs | Real-time | Speech, intent |
| Call ended | Twilio | Backend | Duration, status |
| Transcript | ElevenLabs | Backend | Full conversation |
| Recording | Twilio | Storage | Audio file |
