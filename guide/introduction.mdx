# Welcome to Renchi AI

Renchi AI is an AI-powered voice call handling platform designed for UK HVAC and plumbing service businesses. It handles inbound customer calls with natural conversation, books appointments, and integrates with your existing tools.

## How It Works

1. **Customer calls your business number** - Twilio routes the call to your AI agent
2. **AI agent handles the conversation** - Powered by ElevenLabs, understands context and intent
3. **Actions are taken automatically** - Bookings created, callbacks scheduled, or calls transferred
4. **You stay informed** - Real-time notifications and full conversation transcripts
